# Comprehensive Math Learning Roadmap

> **Goal:** Build a rock-solid foundation from fundamental arithmetic up to advanced STEM/College-level mathematics.

---

## Phase 1: Foundational Fluency

| Step | Course | Stage / Level | Core Focus & Prerequisites |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **01** | **Arithmetic (all content)** | Stage 1: Basics | Place value, fractions, decimals, negative numbers, and mental math fluency. |
| **02** | **Pre-Algebra** | Stage 2: Intro to Variables | Ratios, order of operations, algebraic expressions, and 1-step equations. |

---

## Phase 2: High School Mastery (AÖL Seçmeli 1 & 2 Focus)

| Step | Course | Stage / Level | Core Focus & Prerequisites |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **03** | **Algebra 1** | Stage 3: Core High School | Linear equations/graphs, systems of equations, inequalities, and intro to quadratics. |
| **04** | **High School Geometry** | Stage 4: Spatial Logic | Transformations, proofs, similarity, circles, and right-triangle trigonometry. |
| **05** | **Algebra 2** | Stage 5: Advanced High School | Polynomials, complex numbers, logarithms, exponential growth, and rational functions. <br>*(Primary focus for AÖL Seçmeli Matematik 1)* |
| **06** | **Trigonometry** | Stage 6: Analytic Geometry | Unit circle, trig functions, identities, non-right triangles, and trig equations. <br>*(Primary focus for AÖL Seçmeli Matematik 2)* |
| **07** | **Precalculus** | Stage 7: STEM Gateway | Composite/inverse functions, vectors, matrices, conic sections, and limits intro. |

---

## Phase 3: Introductory College & Engineering Math

| Step | Course | Stage / Level | Core Focus & Prerequisites |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **08** | **Calculus 1** | Stage 8: College Level | Limits, continuity, derivatives, optimization, and fundamental theorem of calculus. |
| **09** | **Calculus 2** | Stage 9: College Level | Advanced integration techniques, Taylor series, parametric/polar equations. |
| **10** | **Linear Algebra** | Stage 10: Advanced STEM | Vectors, matrices, linear transformations, vector spaces, and eigenvalues. <br>*(Crucial for Computer Science, Robotics, and AI)* |

---

## Phase 4: Advanced STEM & Applied Mathematics

| Step | Course | Stage / Level | Core Focus & Prerequisites |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **11** | **Multivariable Calculus** | Stage 11: Advanced College | 3D vectors, partial derivatives, multiple integrals, vector calculus (Green's/Stokes' theorems). |
| **12** | **Differential Equations** | Stage 12: Applied Physics/Eng | First/second-order differential equations, Laplace transforms, and physical systems modeling. |
| **13** | **Statistics and Probability** | Stage 13: Data & Inference | Descriptive/inferential statistics, probability distributions, confidence intervals, hypothesis testing. |
