göç : migration 

- **Karşılıklı yer değiştirme:** Mutual exchange / Swapping places.
    
- **Anlaşmalar:** Agreements/Treaties between countries.
    
- **Zorunlu:** Mandatory/Forced.

Gelişmiş : Development 

- **Hasat:** Harvest.
    
- **İstihdam:** Employment/Jobs.
    
- **Verimlilik:** Productivity/Efficiency.
    
- **İklimsel faktörler:** Climate factors (Rain, sun, temperature).


- **Kuraklık:** Drought (No rain/water).
    
- **İç Anadolu & Güneydoğu Anadolu:** These are the driest, flattest parts of Turkey (Konya, Urfa, etc.).
    
- **Çölleşme (C):** Desertification (Turning into a desert). "Çöl" = Desert.
    
- **Heyelan (A):** Landslide. This happens on **steep mountains** with **lots of rain** (usually the Black Sea region).
    
- **Salgın hastalık (B):** Epidemic/Pandemic (Like COVID-19). "Salgın" = Spreading.
