- **Görsel (Plastik):** Uses physical matter (clay, paint, stone). _Heykel, Resim, Mimari._
    
- **İşitsel (Fonetik):** Uses sound and rhythm. _Edebiyat, Müzik._
    
- **Dramatik (Ritmik):** Uses movement/body. _Tiyatro, Dans, Opera._


- **Olay (Maupassant) Style:** Has a clear beginning-middle-end, high tension, and a "what happens next?" vibe.
    
- **Durum (Çehov) Style:** Just a "slice of life" (_hayattan bir kesit_). No big drama, no strict structure.



- **Lirik:** Feelings (Love, sadness).
    
- **Didaktik:** Teaching/Advice (_nasihat/öğüt_).
    
- **Epik:** War/Heroism.
    
- **Pastoral:** Shepherds/Nature.
    
- **Satirik:** Making fun of something/Criticism.

When adding a suffix to an abbreviation (Kısaltma), you look at the **last letter**.

**DTCF’nden:** This is wrong because it's treating the suffix as if it's the full name "Fakültesi'nden." In Turkish, you ignore the full name. It should be _DTCF'den_.
