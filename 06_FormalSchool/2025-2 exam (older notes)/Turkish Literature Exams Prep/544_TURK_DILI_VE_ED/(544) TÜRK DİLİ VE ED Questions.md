**A) Olay Ahmet Cemil’in, anılarını aktarmasıdır:** (The event is Ahmet Cemil telling his own memories).

- **The Trap:** While he _remembers_ something for a split second, the paragraph is a **Third-Person Narrative**. It says "He felt" (Hissediyordu), "He saw" (Görüyor). He isn't "writing his memoirs" here; a narrator is telling us what Ahmet Cemil is doing right now.
