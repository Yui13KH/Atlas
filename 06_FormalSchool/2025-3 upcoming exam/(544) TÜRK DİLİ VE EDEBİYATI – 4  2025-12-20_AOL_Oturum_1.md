### The Cheat Code: How to Spot a "Giriş Cümlesi" (Entry Sentence)

A perfect entry sentence **cannot** contain "connector" words that refer back to something mentioned previously. If you see these, it’s **not** an entry sentence:

1. **"Bu" (This/These):** e.g., _Bu_ halılar, _bu_ kurslar. (This implies you already talked about the carpets/courses before).
    
2. **"Ve/Yine/Daha" (And/Again/More):** Connectors that link the sentence to a previous thought.
    
3. **"Sonunda/İşte" (Finally/Thus):** Words that imply a conclusion or a continuing narrative.
    

### Logic Breakdown

- **A is FALSE:** It starts with "Mehter," but then says "...sonunda..." (finally/in the end) and "...yine bu dönemde..." (again, in this period). It sounds like the _conclusion_ of a paragraph.
    
- **B is TRUE:** It stands alone perfectly. It introduces a broad subject ("Türk mutfağı") and gives background info without referring to anything mentioned earlier. It is the "main topic" sentence.
    
- **C is FALSE:** It says "açılan **bu** kurslar" (these courses). Which courses? The sentence assumes we already know what courses it's talking about.
    
- **D is FALSE:** It says "**bu** halılar" (these carpets). Again, it refers to something mentioned previously.
    

**Correct Answer: B**

### The Question 3 ) 

**KARAGÖZ : Hayır ( ) demir yolundan bir hayli mesafe gittik.**

**HACİVAT : Vah vah ( ) siz ta Uzunçayır’a kadar çıkmışsınız ( )**

**KARAGÖZ : Ben beygir miyim ki çayıra çıkayım Hacivat?**

**HACİVAT : Beygirsin, demedim ( ) oraya Uzunçayır derler, onu demek istedim.**

**Bu parçada yay ayraçla gösterilen yerlere sırasıyla aşağıdakilerin hangisinde verilen noktalama işaretleri getirilebilir?**

- **A) (,) (,) (!) (;)**
    
- **B) (!) (!) (.) (,)**
    
- **C) (!) (,) (.) (;)**
    
- **D) (,) (!) (!) (,)**
    

### The Logic (Cheat Codes)

1. **Rule of "Hayır":** After words like _Evet_, _Hayır_, or _Peki_ at the start of a sentence, always use a **comma (,)**. This eliminates B and C.
    
2. **Rule of "Vah Vah":** While it sounds like an exclamation, in this specific sentence structure, it acts as an introductory interjection followed by a comma, building up to the exclamation point later.
    
3. **Rule of the Exclamation (!):** "Siz ta Uzunçayır’a kadar çıkmışsınız" expresses high surprise/disbelief from Hacivat, so it demands an **exclamation mark (!)**.
    
4. **Rule of the Semicolon (;):** When you have two complete clauses in one sentence that are closely related but need a distinct separation (like correcting yourself), a **semicolon (;)** is the correct bridge.
    

**Correct Answer: A**

### The Question

**(I) Isparta’da stresten uzaklaşmak, doğayla başbaşa kalmak, rahat bir nefes almak mı istiyorsunuz?**

**(II) O zaman yemyeşil ormanla kaplı Gölcük Tabiat Parkı’na gidebilirsiniz.**

**(III) Isparta’da son zamanlar da keşfedilen bu park, doğal güzelliklere sahip cennetten bir köşe.**

**(IV) Torosların nazar boncuğu olarak anılan park, elli sekiz endemik bitki türüne de ev sahipliği yapıyor.**

**Bu parçadaki numaralanmış cümlelerin hangilerinde yazım yanlışı vardır?**

- **A)** I ve II
    
- **B)** I ve III
    
- **C)** II ve IV
    
- **D)** III ve IV
    

### The Cheat Codes (How to spot these errors quickly)

1. **Sentence I:** "başbaşa" -> This is wrong. When words are reduplicated or used as phrases like this, they must be written separately: **"baş başa"**.
    
2. **Sentence II:** "Gölcük Tabiat Parkı’na" -> This is correct! Proper names (Park names) always take capital letters and apostrophes for suffixes.
    
3. **Sentence III:** "son zamanlar da" -> This is wrong. The "da" here is a _bağlaç_ (conjunction meaning "also/even"), so it must be written separately. **Wait, let's look closer:** "son zamanlarda" is actually a single time phrase meaning "in recent times." In this context, it should be written as one word: **"zamanlarda"**.
    
4. **Sentence IV:** "elli sekiz" -> Numbers are always written separately in Turkish. This is correct.
    

**Errors found:** Sentence I ("baş başa") and Sentence III ("zamanlarda").

**Correct Answer: B**

### Why your intuition was actually good:

- You correctly suspected **"başbaşa"** looked weird (it is!).
    
- You correctly felt **"3"** was suspicious (the "da" error is a classic test trap).
    
- Even if you weren't 100% sure on the rules, your process of elimination based on "gut feeling" is exactly how you handle these spelling questions in an exam.
    

### High-Yield Vocabulary / Grammar Tips

- **Baş başa:** Always two words.
    
- **Bağlaç (Conjunction) "da/de":** If you can remove the "da/de" and the sentence still makes sense, it's a _bağlaç_ and must be separate (e.g., _Isparta da keşfedildi_ -> _Isparta keşfedildi_).
    
- **Hal Eki (Suffix) "-da/-de":** If the sentence loses its meaning without it, it's a suffix and must be attached (e.g., _Isparta'da yaşıyorum_ -> _Isparta yaşıyorum_ [doesn't make sense, so it stays attached]).

**(I) Kozan’a bağlı (Delikkaya) köyündeki (Anavarza), birçok tarihî mekâna ev sahipliği yapan (Çukurova’nın) en (görkemli kale kenti).**

- **A)** I **B)** II **C)** III **D)** IV
    

### The Cheat Code: "Kanıtlanabilirlik" (Proving it)

In Turkish exams, this question asks: **Is it Objective or Subjective?**

- **Objective (Nesnel):** Can be proven with facts, data, or measurements. (e.g., "The wall is 5 meters high.")
    
- **Subjective (Öznel):** Based on personal opinion, feelings, or comparisons. (e.g., "The wall is beautiful.")
    

**How to solve:** Look for adjectives that express _personal opinion_ (beautiful, impressive, magnificent, like a ship).

### Logic Breakdown

- **(I) "Görkemli" (Magnificent):** This is a subjective opinion. Someone else might think it looks small or plain.
    
- **(II) "Sonsuz bir deniz gibi... okyanustaki bir gemiye benzer" (Like an endless sea... like a ship in the ocean):** This is a personal comparison (a metaphor). It is highly subjective.
    
- **(III) "Kalıntılarıyla dolu" (Full of ruins):** This is a factual, objective observation. You can look at the field and count/see the ruins.
    
- **(IV) "Etkileyici" (Impressive):** This is another personal opinion.
    

_Wait!_ Let's look closer. Actually, sentences I, II, and IV all contain strong subjective adjectives (görkemli, benzer/gibi, etkileyici). Sentence III is a **descriptive fact**.

**Correct Answer: C**
