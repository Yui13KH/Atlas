- **Rumlar:** This refers to the **Greeks** living in Turkey (historically from the Byzantine/Ottoman era).
- **Karşılıklı yer değiştirme:** Mutual exchange / Swapping places.

After the Turkish War of Independence, Turkey and Greece signed an agreement to "swap" populations. Turks in Greece moved to Turkey, and Greeks (Rumlar) in Turkey moved to Greece.

In Turkish, the official word for this specific type of "population swap" is **Mübadele**.

- **A) İş gücü göçü (Labor Migration):** This is for work (like people moving to Germany in the 60s). It's usually voluntary.
- **B) Beyin göçü (Brain Drain):** This is when doctors/engineers move for better jobs.
- **D) Doğal afet göçü (Natural Disaster Migration):** Moving because of an earthquake or flood.

**Correct Answer: C (Mübadele göçü)**
