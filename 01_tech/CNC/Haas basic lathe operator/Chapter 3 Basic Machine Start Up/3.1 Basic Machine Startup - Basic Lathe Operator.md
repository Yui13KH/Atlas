# CNC Lathe Startup & Safety Procedure (SOP)

## I. Pre-Power Inspection

- **Main Power Breaker:** Located at the rear of the machine. Ensure it is switched to the **ON** position.
    
    > _Safety Note:_ Keep the electrical cabinet closed at all times to prevent high-voltage exposure.
    
- **Pneumatic Check:** Inspect the air pressure gauge. The machine requires a steady supply between **85–115 PSI** to operate the tool changer and air blasts.
    
- **Environmental Check:** Ensure the area around the chip conveyor and coolant tank is clear of spills or obstructions.
    

## II. Power-On Sequence

1. **Power On:** Press the [POWER ON] button on the control pendant.
    
2. **Safety Diagnostics:** The [E-STOP] light will blink.
    
3. **Cycle the Door:** Open and close the operator door. This confirms the door sensors and interlocks are functioning correctly.
    
4. **Release E-Stop:** Rotate the [EMERGENCY STOP] button clockwise to release it.
    
5. **Clear Alarms:** Press the [RESET] button to clear startup alarms.
    

## III. Homing & Tool Clearance

1. **Homing Sequence:** Press [POWER UP]. The machine will begin its "Zero Return" (moving to machine home).
    
2. **Collision Check:** **Crucial Step.** Physically look inside the machine. Ensure no long tools in the turret will strike the chuck, workpiece, or tailstock during rotation or travel.
    
3. **Manual Intervention:** If a collision is possible, use the **Hand Jog** wheel to move the turret to a safe clearance zone before finishing the homing process.
    
4. **Axis Reset:** * Press **[X]** then **[HOME G28]** (X-axis usually homes first to clear the diameter).

## IV. Post-Startup Maintenance & Prep

- **Cleanliness:** Remove any chip clumps.
    
    - _Safety Rule:_ Always wear heavy-duty gloves when handling chips; they are razor-sharp. Never check sharpness with bare fingers.
        
- **Coolant Management:** 1. Check the digital levels in the [MEMORY] or [DIAGNOSTIC] screen.
    
    2. Check the graphical display for a quick reference.
    
    3. **Physical Verification:** Visually check the coolant tank in the rear to confirm the sensor is accurate.
    
- **Chip Tray:** Inspect the chip catch tray and clear any debris to ensure proper coolant drainage.
    

## V. Emergency & Motion Controls

Always keep your hand near these three buttons during a new program "First Run":

|**Control**|**Action**|**When to Use**|
|---|---|---|
|**Feed Hold**|Stops all axis movement; **Spindle stays on**.|To check a measurement or if you are unsure of the next move.|
|**Reset**|Stops motion and resets the G-code to line 1.|To abort a program that isn't running correctly.|
|**E-Stop**|Kills all power and motion immediately.|**In case of imminent or actual crash.**|
