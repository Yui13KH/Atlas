## 📋 Phase 4: Coolant 101

- [ ] **Introduction to Coolant 101**: Titan introduces John Hinders to discuss why water-soluble metalworking fluids are the liquid tool of machining.
    
- [ ] **Water Quality**: Quality water equals quality coolant. Learn how water hardness and mineral content impact emulsion stability.
    
- [ ] **What is pH?**: Learn why maintaining correct pH levels prevents corrosion, skin irritation, and bacterial growth in the coolant tank.
    
- [ ] **Types of Coolant**: Learn the differences between straight oils, soluble oils, semi-synthetics, and full synthetics.
    
- [ ] **Vasco 7000**: Learn how high-performance vegetable-oil based coolants handle tough alloys and heavy tool pressures.
    
- [ ] **How to Mix Coolant**: Always add oil to water, never water to oil! Learn proper mixing methods for stable emulsions.
    
- [ ] **What is a Refractometer?**: Learn how to calibrate and use an optical refractometer to measure coolant concentration percentage.
    
- [ ] **Concentration Control**: Learn why maintaining the correct concentration ratio protects cutting tools and prevents rust.
    
- [ ] **Oil Skimming**: Learn how disk and belt oil skimmers remove tramp oil floating on top of the coolant sump.
    
- [ ] **Tramp Oil**: Understand how hydraulic oil and slide way lube leak into your coolant and degrade machine performance.
    
- [ ] **Maintain Sump, Don't Dump**: Good housekeeping tips for extending coolant life, filtering chips, and maintaining sump cleanliness.
    
- [ ] **Rotten Eggs & Dirty Socks**: Learn how anaerobic bacteria cause foul sump odors and how to prevent bacterial blooms.
