# Shop Safety Guidelines: Quick Reference Guide for New Operators

## Key Safety Rules & Procedures

- **Review Rules with Supervisor:** Always go over your shop's specific rules and regulations with your supervisor before starting work and memorize them.
    
- **Keep Safety Doors Closed:** Always keep machine safety doors shut during operation to prevent flying debris from exiting and causing injury.
    
- **Manage Entanglement Hazards:**
    
    - Tie back long hair.
        
    - Wear short sleeves (avoid long sleeves).
        
    - Remove all jewelry and any other items that risk being caught in a moving spindle.
        
- **Machines Are Dangerous:** These high-powered machines are extremely dangerous and can cause severe injury or death; proceed with extreme caution at all times.
    
- **Stop and Ask for Help:** If you feel uncomfortable at any point, stop what you are doing immediately and ask for assistance.
    
- **Contribute to Safety:** If you notice anything that needs to be added to improve safety, talk to your supervisor to update the shop's regulations and ensure a safe environment for everyone.
