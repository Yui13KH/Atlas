# Using a Magnetic Vise Stop: Quick Reference Guide for New Operators

## Step-by-Step Procedure

- **1. Adjust Pin Length:** Use the cam lever to adjust the stop pin length. Moving the lever toward the middle unlocks the pin for length adjustments; moving it to the down position locks the pin firmly in place. Set the length so your stock is positioned directly in the center of the vise.
    
- **2. Clean Mating Surfaces:** Ensure both the bottom of the magnetic stop and the mounting surface on the vise are completely clean and free of chips or debris.
    
- **3. Mount the Magnetic Stop:** Place the magnetic stop flat against the mounting surface, ensuring it sits completely flush and is not cocked or resting on any other surfaces.
    
- **4. Position the Stock:** Insert your raw material into the vise and **lightly bump** it up against the stop to avoid shifting the stop position.
    
- **5. Check Height & Clearance:** Verify the height where the stop contacts the part. Position it near the bottom to avoid tool collisions if machining around the part, or **remove the stop entirely** before running the machining program.
