
# Loading Material into a Vise: Quick Reference Guide for New Operators

## Key Best Practices

- **Center Your Material:** Always place your raw stock right in the **center of the vise**.
    
- **Avoid Off-Center Clamping:** If a part is clamped off to one side, it will pinch the vise unevenly. This results in improper holding force and can cause the material to **lift out of the vise** during heavy machining.
    
- **Maximize Holding Force:** Centering the part ensures the most even distribution of clamping pressure and provides the most secure grip possible.
