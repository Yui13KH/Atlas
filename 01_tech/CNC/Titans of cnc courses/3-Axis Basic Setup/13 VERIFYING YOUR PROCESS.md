# Final Process Verification & First Run: Quick Reference Guide for New Operators

## Step-by-Step Procedure

- **1. Remove the Z Dry Run Offset:** Go back to the **Offset** page, select your active work coordinate system (**G54**), navigate to the **Z column fine adjustment** tab, and change the **6-inch** offset back to **0** so tools return to their correct cutting height.
    
- **2. Check Rigidity:** Inspect your entire setup to ensure everything is rigid and secure:
    
    - Confirm the work stop is rigid.
        
    - Verify the part is clamped firmly and properly in the vise.
        
    - Ensure all cutting tools are securely loaded and rigid in the spindle. _(Rigidity is the absolute key to successful machining)._
        
- **3. Prep for First Cut:**
    
    - Turn your **coolant back on** (if it was turned off for the dry run).
        
    - Turn down your **rapid overrides** on the control.
        
    - Keep your hand ready on the **Feed Hold** (cycle stop) button so you can pause the machine at any moment.
        
- **4. Verify Z-Height Live:** As the tool comes down toward the part for the very first time, use **Feed Hold** when it gets close. Check the active Z-height coordinates on the control screen to visually and mathematically verify the tool is at the correct height before resuming.
    
- **5. Hit Cycle Start:** Resume the program, monitor closely, and you are ready to successfully make chips!
