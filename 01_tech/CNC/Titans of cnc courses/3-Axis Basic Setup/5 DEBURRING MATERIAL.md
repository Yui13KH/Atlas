# Material Preparation & Deburring: Quick Reference Guide for New Operators

## Step-by-Step Procedure

- **1. Inspect and Clean Raw Stock:** Ensure raw material is completely free of burrs and debris before placing it into the vise to guarantee even clamping force across the entire part.
    
- **2. Deburr Using a File (Method 1):**
    
    - Push the file up and down along the edges to remove sharp burrs.
        
    - Repeat this process on all sides of the material where the vise jaws will clamp.
        
- **3. Deburr Using a Burr Whip (Method 2):**
    
    - Pull the tool toward you with a "whip" motion over the corners.
        
    - Work your way around the entire part to ensure all clamped surfaces and corners are clean and smooth.
        
- **4. Load into Vise:** Once fully deburred and clean, place the material securely onto your parallels inside the vise and clamp it down.
