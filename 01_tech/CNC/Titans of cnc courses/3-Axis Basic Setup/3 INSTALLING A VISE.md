# Vise Stoning and Installation: Quick Reference Guide for New Operators

## Step-by-Step Procedure

- **1. Clean the Back of the Vise:** Ensure the bottom/back of the vise is free of all chips and debris using a rag, then finish by wiping with your **bare hand** to feel for any minor particles.
    
- **2. Stone the Vise (Rough Side):** Lightly spray the back of the vise with WD-40. Use the **rough side** of your stone in small circles or figure-eight patterns across all surfaces.
    
- **3. Stone the Vise (Fine Side):** Switch to the **fine side** of the stone and repeat the same figure-eight / circular motion.
    
- **4. Re-clean the Vise:** Clean the back of the vise again with a rag followed by a final check with your hand.
    
- **5. Clean the CNC Table:** Thoroughly clean the machine table using a rag, followed by checking with your hand to ensure zero debris.
    
- **6. Stone the CNC Table:** Spray the table lightly with WD-40, then stone the entire surface using light pressure in figure-eight or circular patterns (rough side first, then fine side).
    
- **7. Re-clean the Table:** Clean the table surface again with a towel and verify with your hand.
    
- **8. Place the Vise:** Carefully and lightly set the vise down onto the table as square as possible by eye to avoid chipping the table.
    
- **9. Install Hardware:** Slide the T-nuts under the table slots and thread the bolts in. Make one side hand-tight/wrench-tight to act as a **pivot point**.
    
- **10. Dial In the Vise (Indicator):**
    
    - Bring a dial indicator down to the back fixed jaw of the vise.
        
    - Zero out the indicator on one side, then traverse across the X-axis to check alignment.
        
    - Gently tap the vise with a soft mallet to make minor adjustments until it reads zero across the entire travel.
        
- **11. Lock Down and Re-Check:** Fully tighten both vise mounting bolts down, then **re-indicate** to ensure the tightening process didn't pull the vise out of alignment.
