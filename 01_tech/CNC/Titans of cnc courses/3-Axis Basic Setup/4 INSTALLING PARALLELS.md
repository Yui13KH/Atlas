# Installing Locking Parallels: Quick Reference Guide for New Operators

## Step-by-Step Procedure

- **1. Ensure Clean Surfaces:** Clean all mating surfaces thoroughly, including the inside of the vise jaws and the bottoms of the parallels.
    
- **2. Check Dimensions:** Verify your measurements (e.g., matching parallel height against jaw height to calculate the exact holding area specified on your setup sheet).
    
- **3. Install Locking Parallels:** Slide the specialized locking parallels into the jaw slots so they are held firmly in place, preventing movement and keeping chips from getting underneath.
    
    - _Note for Loose Parallels:_ If using standard loose parallels, use a securing method (such as rubber bands, spring clamps, or strap material) to hold them down and block chips.
        
- **4. Removing Parallels:** Use an Allen wrench in the designated "kick-out" screw hole on the jaws to push the parallel out when removal is necessary.
    
- **5. Confirm Setup Sheet:** Take a measurement from the top of the jaws to the parallels to confirm it matches your setup sheet and gives you the correct holding area.
    
- **6. Final Clean & Load:** Ensure the top of the parallels are completely clean and your part is deburred before loading the part into the vise.
