
# Tightening a Vise: Quick Reference Guide for New Operators

## Step-by-Step Procedure

- **1. Use Standard Handle for Initial Closing:** Use your standard vise handle to open and close the vise, bringing the jaws in to get a **light lock** on the part.
    
- **2. Switch to a Torque Wrench:** Because different operators have varying physical strengths, standard handles can lead to inconsistent clamping force. Switch to a preset **torque wrench** to ensure the exact same locking force is applied every time.
    
- **3. Apply Final Torque:** Tighten the vise using the torque wrench until you hear a **loud click**, which indicates that the correct, preset clamping force has been reached and the part is properly secured.
