#### **Question**

> İlk sayısı 16 Mart 1950’de çıkan Hisar **( )** son sayısının çıktığı Aralık 1980’e kadar **( )** ki bu dönem edebiyatımızın en çalkantılı dönemidir **( )** aralıklı olarak yirmi üç yıl yayımlanmış bir dergidir **( )**
> 
> **Bu cümlede yay ayraçla gösterilen yerlere gelmesi gereken noktalama işaretleri aşağıdakilerin hangisinde sırasıyla verilmiştir?**
> 
> **A)** (,) (—) (—) (.)
> **B)** (;) (,) (,) (!)
> **C)** (;) (:) (,) (.)
> **D)** (,) (“) (”) (!)

---

#### **Target Analysis**

- **Correct Answer:** **A**
    
- **Question Type:** #noktalama (Punctuation)
    
- **Difficulty:** Medium
    

#### **Reasoning**

1. **Gap 1 (The Subject):** "Hisar" is the subject (özne). In Turkish, we usually put a **Comma (,)** after the subject to separate it from the rest of the action.
    
2. **Gap 2 & 3 (The Interruption):** The phrase _"...ki bu dönem edebiyatımızın en çalkantılı dönemidir..."_ is an **Ara Söz** (Extra explanation).
    
    - **Rule:** Extra explanations must be "hugged" by either **two commas** or **two long dashes (—)**.
        
    - Looking at Option A, it uses dashes. This fits perfectly.
        
3. **Gap 4 (The End):** It is a standard statement sentence. It needs a **Period (.)**.
    

#### **The "Non-Native" Cheat Sheet**

- **Look for "Ara Söz":** If you see a sentence-inside-a-sentence (usually starting with _ki_ or _yani_), immediately look for the double dash `— ... —` or double comma `, ... ,` in the options.
    
- **Subject Marker:** The first comma in Turkish sentences is almost always there just to tell you "Hey, the thing before this comma is what the whole sentence is about."
    

---

> [!TIP] **Strategy for Noktalama**
> 
> When I see four brackets, I check the **last one first**. If the sentence is just a normal sentence, I eliminate all options that don't end with a dot (.). This usually removes 50% of the wrong answers immediately!

#### **Question**

> **Aşağıdaki cümlelerin hangisinde yazım yanlışı vardır?**
> 
> **A)** Yapılan kazılarda Doğu Türkistan’da 8. yüzyıla ait çok sayıda yazılı eser bulunmuştur.
> **B)** Türk dünyasında İslam öncesi siyasi düşünceyi şekillendiren kaynakların başında Orhun Kitabeleri gelmektedir.
> **C)** Türk devletleri, konar göçer yaşamın zorluğunu yönetim sistemlerindeki disiplin sayesinde aşmışlardır.
> **D)** Ahmet Yesevi’nin düşünce ikliminde yol alan Türklerin tasavvuf düşüncesi 13. yüzyılda en verimli dönemine ulaşmıştır.

---

#### **Target Analysis**

- **Correct Answer:** **C**
    
- **Question Type:** #yazım-yanlışı (Spelling Error)
    
- **Difficulty:** Hard (Requires specific vocabulary knowledge)
    

#### **Reasoning**

1. **The Error (C):** The word **"konar göçer"** (nomadic) is an established compound term in Turkish.
    
    - **Rule:** If two words combine to form a specific type of lifestyle or a biological species name, they are usually written **combined**.
        
    - **Correct Spelling:** _konargöçer_.
        
2. **Why A is Correct:** "Doğu Türkistan." Directions (East, West, etc.) are **Capitalized** if they come before a place name.
    
3. **Why B is Correct:** "Orhun Kitabeleri." Specific historical monuments are proper nouns and must be capitalized.
    
4. **Why D is Correct:** "13. yüzyılda." Centuries use a dot for "th" and the suffix is correctly separated by an apostrophe.
    

#### **The "Non-Native" Cheat Sheet**

- **Compound Word Trap:** In Açık Lise, they love to test words that look like they should be separate but are actually joined.
    
- **Direction Rule:** * **Doğu** Anadolu (Capital - it's a specific region)
    
    - Anadolu'nun **doğusu** (Lowercase - it's just a direction)
        
- **The "Yüzyıl" (Century) Shortcut:** Always look for `yüzyıl` or `yy.`. If you see `13. yy'da`, it is usually correct.

3. **Bam teline basmak:** "Stepping on the sensitive string." It means specifically doing/saying the one thing that makes someone very angry.
    
    - Matches with **B** (Birinin çok kızacağı şeyi yapmak).
        

**Option D** ("Not giving importance to something") has no match here. The idiom for D would be something like _"İpinde olmamak"_ or _"Kulak asmamak."_

---

#### **The "Non-Native" Cheat Sheet**

Idioms are the hardest part of a new language because the words don't mean what they say. Use these **Mental Images**:

- **Baltayı taşa vurmak:** Imagine an axe (speech) hitting a rock (someone's sensitivity). It’s an **Accident/Mistake**.
    
- **Bana mısın dememek:** Imagine pushing a wall and the wall is just chilling. It’s about **Strength/Resistance**.
    
- **Bam teline basmak:** Imagine a guitar string that, if touched, makes a loud angry noise. It’s about **Provocation**.

> [!TIP] **Matching Idioms (Deyim Eşleştirme)**
>

>     
> 2. Match _Bam teli_ $\rightarrow$ _Kızmak_ (Anger).
>     
> 3. Match _Baltayı taşa vurmak_ $\rightarrow$ _Hata/Dokunmak_ (Oops/Mistake).
>     
> 4. Match _Bana mısın dememek_ $\rightarrow$ _Etkisiz_ (No effect).

### **[Söz Sanatları (Literary Devices)]**

#### **Question**

> Dönmüş bugün çöllerden
> 
> Bulurum diyerek Mecnun
> 
> Leylasının düşüyle
> 
> **Çatıları göğe değen** > Bir kentin eteğine
> 
> **Bu şiirde aşağıdaki söz sanatlarından hangisi kullanılmıştır?**
> 
> **A)** Tezat
> 
> **B)** Mübalağa
> 
> **C)** Teşhis
> 
> **D)** Hüsnütalil

---

#### **Target Analysis**

- **Correct Answer:** **B**
    
- **Question Type:** `#söz-sanatları` (Literary Devices)
    
- **Difficulty:** Medium (Requires identifying exaggeration)
    

#### **Reasoning**

1. **Why B is the Answer (Mübalağa):** Look at the bolded line: **"Çatıları göğe değen"** (Roofs that touch the sky).
    
    - This is a classic **Exaggeration** (Mübalağa). Even the tallest skyscraper doesn't actually touch the sky/heavens. The poet is exaggerating the height of the city buildings to emphasize how overwhelming or "modern" the city looks compared to Mecnun's desert.
        
2. **Why it's NOT D (Hüsnütalil):** _Hüsnütalil_ means "Beautiful reason." It’s when you attribute a natural event to a poetic cause (e.g., "The clouds are crying because you left"). This poem doesn't assign a fake "reason" to a natural event; it just describes the city as impossibly tall.
    
3. **Why it's NOT A (Tezat):** No opposing concepts (like black/white or life/death) are used to create contrast.
    
4. **Why it's NOT C (Teşhis):** This is personification (giving human traits to objects). The roofs aren't acting like humans; they are just "tall."
    

---

#### **The "Non-Native" Cheat Sheet**

You don't need the whole story of Mecnun and Leyla. Look for **Impossible Physical Descriptions**:

- **Mübalağa (Exaggeration):** Look for things that are physically impossible in size or scale.
    
    - _Example:_ "I cried enough to fill a sea" or "**Roofs touching the sky.**"
        
- **Hüsnütalil (Poetic Reason):** Look for a "because."
    
    - _Example:_ "The sun came out **because** you smiled."
        
- **Teşhis (Personification):** Look for objects doing "human" things.
    
    - _Example:_ "The tired old road."
        

---

### **Your Obsidian Journal Entry**

> [!IMPORTANT] **Söz Sanatları Triggers**
> 
> - **Exaggerated Size/Scale** $\rightarrow$ **Mübalağa**.
>     
> - **Nature + "Because"** $\rightarrow$ **Hüsnütalil**.
>     
> - **Objects acting human** $\rightarrow$ **Teşhis**.
>     
> - _Açık Lise Hack:_ If you see words like "göğe değmek" (touching the sky), "dünyalar kadar" (as much as worlds), or "dağlar devirmek" (toppling mountains), the answer is always **Mübalağa**.

### **[Eser-Yazar-Tür (Author-Work-Genre)]**

#### **Question**

> **Rasim Özdenören’e ait aşağıdaki eserlerden hangisi deneme türünde kaleme alınmıştır?**
> 
> **A)** Denize Açılan Kapı
> 
> **B)** Ruhun Malzemeleri
> 
> **C)** Ansızın Yola Çıkmak
> 
> **D)** Gül Yetiştiren Adam

---

#### **Target Analysis**

- **Correct Answer:** **B**
    
- **Question Type:** `#yazar-eser-eşleştirme` (Author-Work Match)
    
- **Difficulty:** High (Pure memorization)
    

#### **Reasoning**

1. **B) Ruhun Malzemeleri:** This is a famous **Deneme** (Essay) book by Rasim Özdenören. He discusses culture, soul, and society.
    
2. **A & C (Story):** _Denize Açılan Kapı_ and _Ansızın Yola Çıkmak_ are actually **Hikâye** (Story) books.
    
3. **D (Novel):** _Gül Yetiştiren Adam_ is his most famous work, but it is a **Roman** (Novel). It’s a classic "trap" choice because everyone knows the name, but forgets the genre.
    

---

#### **The "Non-Native" Cheat Sheet**

When dealing with Rasim Özdenören, memorize this "Genre Triangle":

- **Novel:** _Gül Yetiştiren Adam_
    
- **Essays (Deneme):** _Ruhun Malzemeleri_, _Müslümanca Düşünme Üzerine Denemeler_.
    
- **Stories:** _Hastalar ve Işıklar_, _Çözülme_.
